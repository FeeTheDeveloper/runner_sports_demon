# Music campaign framework

Verify the available Google music surface, supported model/features, duration and usage terms at execution time. “Flow Music” in the draft is a workflow label, not proof of an available app or account entitlement.

Prepare a portable original music brief: campaign/entity; duration target; tempo range; instrumentation; groove; energy curve; intro/hook/build/end; vocals or instrumental; approved original lyrics; sound palette; loop and cut-down requirements.
Example: “Original instrumental streetwear launch bed, relaxed confident groove, tight drums, warm bass, airy synth accents. Immediate rhythmic hook, space for voiceover, build into the product reveal, clean final hit for CTA. No vocals.”
Describe musical traits rather than copying an artist or song. Use owner-provided tracks only with documented rights. Never infer permission for voice cloning.

Align musical accents with shot transitions. Prepare 15- and 30-second edit targets only when requested; verify generation length and perform cuts in an authorized editor. Preserve the master plus cut versions and record model, prompt, output and approval evidence.
Check clipping, abrupt edits, intelligibility, voiceover masking, unwanted lyrics and continuity. Current official terms determine permitted use; never certify legal clearance from a prompt.
