## Identity

- **Domain / CTA default:** werunsportsandanalytics.com
- **What it is:** Sports intelligence — game research, analysis, alerts, and reporting across NFL, NBA, WNBA, MLB, NHL, NCAAF, and men's NCAAB. Focus areas: totals, game flow, live research, Live Desk.
- **Core equation:** Sports Data + Modeling + Market Intelligence + Live Game Flow + Price Discipline + Accountability + Presentation.
- **Audience:** Sports fans and researchers who want analysis and decision support, not touts.
- **Voice:** Sharp, analytical, energetic. Data-forward, never a tout. Proprietary technical sports-intelligence posture.
- **Palette direction:** Red, blue, and white (LOCK §15). Exact hex values and the primary/secondary split: `CONFIRM`. Until confirmed, prompt for red/blue/white lighting and set dressing and add exact brand color in post.
- **Mark:** Large "R" icon direction with integrated sports elements (LOCK §15). Add the real mark in post; never ask the model to draw it.
- **Visual world:** Data visuals over generic sport action; scoreboard-style motion graphics added in post; anonymous athletes in unbranded uniforms; stadium light; analyst desk. Fast cuts, snap zooms. Avoid generic templates. Analytical information must remain readable without sponsor elements.
- **Music direction:** High-tempo, percussive, broadcast-energy bed. Instrumental.
- **Best formats:** 9:16 game-day hype and slate previews; 16:9 explainer of how the intelligence works; 16:9 site hero loops.

## Guardrails (strict)

- Intelligence, alerts, and analysis only. No "locks", guaranteed wins, promised returns, or win-rate claims.
- Never present research as a guaranteed financial or wagering outcome. Never fabricate stats, picks, results, customers, or model output in captions or on screen; real numbers come from King Fee or the verified live product, with source timestamps preserved.
- No real players, team names, team or league logos, broadcast marks, or sportsbook marks in generated video or stills. Use generic, unbranded athletes and venues.
- Sponsor identity, CTA, QR, logos, and disclosures stay separable from analytical content. Never imply sponsor influence on analysis.
- Public certification claims (veteran-owned, VOSB, SDVOSB, minority-owned, woman-owned) require supporting documentation or authoritative verification before they appear in a campaign.
- `CONFIRM` whether captions need a responsible-play or age disclaimer on each platform and in each state being marketed; do not assert a requirement without current official evidence.
