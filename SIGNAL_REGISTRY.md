Totals signal families: `TOTAL_OVER_BUILDING`, `TOTAL_UNDER_BUILDING`, `TEAM_TOTAL_OVER_BUILDING`, `TEAM_TOTAL_UNDER_BUILDING`, `HALFTIME_OVER_WINDOW`, `HALFTIME_UNDER_WINDOW`, `SECOND_HALF_OVER_WINDOW`, `SECOND_HALF_UNDER_WINDOW`, `QUARTER_OVER_WINDOW`, `QUARTER_UNDER_WINDOW`, `SCORING_CONVERSION_SUPPRESSION`, `SCORING_REGRESSION_CANDIDATE`, `PACE_ACCELERATION`, `PACE_COLLAPSE`, `CLOCK_DRAIN`, `RED_ZONE_SUPPRESSION`, `GARBAGE_TIME_OVER`, `GARBAGE_TIME_UNDER`, `TOTAL_MARKET_LAG`, `TOTAL_MARKET_OVERREACTION`, and `TOTAL_MARKET_UNDERREACTION`.

Signals are evidence labels, never wagering instructions. A signal becomes a user-facing decision window only after selection-aligned edge, confidence, freshness, synchronization, and suppression checks.
